PurpleStars School Portal

Transforming fragmented academic and administrative processes into one role-aware school operations platform.

  • Multi-stakeholder workflow analysis
  • Role-based functional requirements
  • Process and data model definition

Business problem

Schools often depend on disconnected spreadsheets, paper records and messaging tools. This causes duplicated work, delayed information and weak visibility across academic operations.

Stakeholders

Administrators, teachers, students, school leadership, parents and support teams.

Analysis approach

  • Mapped academic setup, attendance, lesson, assessment and result workflows.
  • Separated administrator, teacher and student permissions.
  • Defined core entities and approval states.
  • Created user stories, acceptance criteria and UAT scenarios.

Key requirements

  • Role-based access to academic and administrative functions.
  • Attendance, lesson resources, assignments, assessments and results.
  • Approval before publication of sensitive results.
  • Audit records and protected student data.

Recommended success measures

Process completion, assessment-to-result cycle time, adoption and data quality. These are proposed measures, not claimed production results.

BugBrief AI

Reducing defect-reporting friction by turning browser context and user notes into developer-ready bug reports.

  • Problem and MVP definition
  • Input, output and exception requirements
  • Privacy and AI workflow considerations

Business problem

Many bug reports omit the URL, environment, screenshot, expected behaviour or reproduction context, creating repeated clarification between users, QA teams and developers.

Stakeholders

End users, QA analysts, developers, product managers, support teams and privacy reviewers.

Analysis and AI engineering approach

  • Defined a user-triggered capture flow.
  • Specified screenshot, URL, title, timestamp, browser details and notes.
  • Designed the backend LLM prompt and structured report output.
  • Separated browser capture from secure backend AI processing.
  • Included review, PDF export, mock mode and error handling.

Key requirements

  • Capture only after explicit user action.
  • Generate a structured report from context and notes.
  • Keep AI provider keys out of the extension.
  • Disclose what data is sent to the backend.

Recommended success measures

Report completeness, clarification rate, reporting time and reproduction success.

Cali Academy

Reframing a training academy website around trust, course discovery and a clearer lead-conversion journey.

  • Website and funnel assessment
  • Customer journey redesign
  • Content hierarchy and CTA requirements

Business problem

Prospective students must quickly understand the academy offer, programme credibility, learner outcomes and the next action to take.

Stakeholders

Prospective students, academy leadership, trainers, admissions, marketing and regulatory stakeholders.

Analysis approach

  • Reviewed the conversion path and information gaps.

Key requirements

  • Programme topics, audience and outcomes.
  • Visible credibility and safety information.
  • Easy-to-find enquiry and enrolment actions.

Recommended success measures

Lead conversion, programme engagement, CTA performance and mobile completion.

ONLINE PROGRAMMESBuild career-ready skills from anywhere.
Higher educationInformation architecture

CIMT College

Improving programme discovery and the application journey for prospective online students.

  • Information architecture review
  • Programme discovery requirements
  • Accessibility, SEO and responsive needs

Business problem

Dense programme catalogues and disconnected funding or support information make it harder for prospective students to choose a suitable path and apply.

Stakeholders

Prospective students, admissions, programme owners, financial-aid teams, marketing and student services.

Analysis approach

  • Grouped content around learner intent and programme categories.
  • Simplified primary navigation and deeper programme discovery.
  • Elevated financial-aid pathways.
  • Specified SEO, structured data and accessibility requirements.

Key requirements

  • Browse programmes by subject or career category.
  • Direct routes to funding, support, contact and applications.
  • Persistent and distinct Apply Now action.
  • Accessible desktop and mobile navigation.

Recommended success measures

Programme discovery time, application starts, funding engagement and organic visibility.
