01 · Business problem

High onboarding volume does not matter if customers never activate.

A digital wallet may attract strong sign-up traffic and still lose customers during profile capture, identity verification, KYC checks or manual review.

Transformation thesis: remove friction, not control. The objective is to make the compliant path easier to understand and complete.
Drop-offToo many customers abandon before activation.
Slow recoveryUsers do not know how to correct verification errors.
Manual loadOperations teams spend time on avoidable exceptions.

High-friction zones often combine technical, policy and communication causes: duplicate data entry, unclear validation errors, late eligibility checks, network recovery gaps and opaque manual-review states.

Duplicate data entryCustomers retype information the business already has.
Unclear verification errorsFailure messages do not explain the corrective action.
Late validationDocument issues are discovered after several steps.
Poor network recoveryUsers restart instead of resuming safely.
Manual exception handlingOperations review preventable edge cases.
No funnel visibilityTeams lack a reliable view of where customers abandon.

Design principles: validate as early as possible, communicate what happened in plain language, preserve progress, route genuine exceptions intelligently and expose funnel performance.

05 · Requirements

Make the experience testable — and the controls explicit.

Functional requirements

  • Create account and capture consent.
  • Capture only necessary customer profile data.
  • Validate identity inputs before submission where possible.
  • Support document upload and image-quality checks.
  • Route verification exceptions using defined business rules.
  • Allow customers to save progress and resume safely.
  • Show understandable onboarding status and next action.

Control / non-functional requirements

  • Protect personal and identity data.
  • Maintain traceability of verification and review actions.
  • Provide controlled retry and rate-limiting behaviour.
  • Handle degraded networks and provider outages gracefully.
  • Ensure accessibility and mobile usability.
  • Expose reliable analytics events across the funnel.

Example user story

As a new customer, I want to understand why my ID failed so I can correct it without restarting.

Acceptance: when validation fails, the interface explains the specific problem in user-friendly language, highlights the field or document that needs attention and preserves previously completed steps.
